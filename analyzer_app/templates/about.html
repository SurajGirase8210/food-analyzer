About This Project

The Food Analyzer is an AI-powered web application designed to estimate the nutritional content of food items from images. Users can upload a food photo or use the camera to instantly receive calorie and macro predictions. This project is part of a final-year engineering program and aims to combine technology with health awareness.

🎯 Objective

Helping users maintain a healthy lifestyle by providing fast, convenient, and accurate nutrition estimation powered by AI.

🧠 Technology Stack

Django, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Machine Learning and AI models trained for food recognition and nutrition estimation.

👨‍💻 Developed By

Suraj · Dhiraj · Ninad · Jayesh
Final Year Students

💡 Motivation

Many people struggle to track calories accurately. Our system simplifies this by using image analysis and nutrition prediction to help support healthier decisions.

⚙️ How It Works

  • Upload a food image or use live camera
  • AI detects the food category
  • Nutrition values retrieved from dataset
  • Instant results shown to the user

🧩 Key Features

  • Food image recognition
  • Calorie & macro estimation
  • Manual nutrition entry
  • Camera capture support
  • Responsive UI

🚀 Future Scope

  • Multi-food plate recognition
  • User login with calorie history
  • Smart diet recommendations
  • Daily nutrition tracking dashboard
